2. align=right

The orange square floats right, so this paragraph is the mirror image of the one above: lines end at the left edge of the float and the RIGHT margin is the one that steps inward. Wrapping bugs that only affect one side will show up here. Keep the filler coming: two floats, three floats, a dozen floats, the browser of 1996 handled them all with a shrug.
