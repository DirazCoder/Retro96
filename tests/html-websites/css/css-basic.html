Text align and indent

align=left: this line hugs the left margin.

align=right: this line is pushed to the right edge.

align=center: this line floats in the middle.

align=justify: both edges of this paragraph must be flush, which requires stretching the word spacing on every line but the last; the classic justification test needs at least a couple of lines of prose to stretch, so here they are, marching along, waiting to be justified.

Indented 2em: the first line of this paragraph starts deeper than the rest.

Indented 30px: same idea with pixels.

Hanging indent -1.5em: the first line hangs out to the LEFT of the paragraph body, which is pulled in by matching padding.

Line height

line-height 1: the lines of this paragraph are packed as tightly as the font allows; descenders may nearly touch.

line-height 2: the lines of this paragraph are spread twice as far apart as the font's natural spacing, giving a very airy feel to this otherwise dull filler text.

line-height 150%: one and a half times the normal leading, somewhere between the two paragraphs above.

line-height 8px: the lines OVERLAP each other on purpose. They must remain visible (smeared), not disappear.
